Good to know

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is Pulse of Goa?

Pulse of Goa is an international community for Psytrance fans and people who believe in the power of music, cultural expression and positive action.

How much does membership cost?

Membership is $9.90 per month. Any applicable service fees or additional charges are displayed by the checkout before payment.

Where can I access the community?

Members connect through our private Telegram community. Access instructions are provided through the membership onboarding process.

Does my membership support humanitarian causes?

The community is built to support humanitarian and cultural initiatives through a proposed allocation model. Actual allocations and initiative updates will be communicated transparently.

Can I choose what kind of initiative I support?

Members can express their preferences for humanitarian or cultural initiatives. These preferences help guide community priorities but do not guarantee a direct transfer to a specific organization.

Is this only for people living in Goa?

Absolutely not. Pulse of Goa is a global community. Psytrance connects people across borders, cultures and countries.
